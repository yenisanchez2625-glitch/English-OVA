Food language note: we can talk about foods we eat often and foods we eat sometimes. A balanced diet includes variety.

Stage 2 • Modeling and deconstruction

Explore the story 🐛

Follow the main events. This is a short retelling, not the full text of the book.

🥚First

A tiny caterpillar comes out of an egg.

🍎 🍐 🍓 🍊Then

It eats fruit on different days.

🍰 🍦 🍬One day

It eats many other foods and gets a stomachache.

🍃After that

It eats a green leaf and feels better.

🟢Next

It builds a cocoon around itself.

🦋Finally

It becomes a beautiful butterfly.

Notice the language!
First, the caterpillar ate an apple. Then, it ate a pear. Finally, it became a butterfly.

Stage 3 • Joint construction

Let’s write together! ✏️

A recount tells events in the order they happened. Use time words and past-tense verbs.

Complete the sentences

Choose the missing word for each sentence.

1. First, the caterpillar ___ an apple.

2. Then, it ate a ___.

3. Finally, it became a ___.

Our class model
First, the caterpillar came out of the egg. Then, it ate fruit and other foods. After that, it made a cocoon. Finally, it became a butterfly.
Stage 4 • Independent construction

Create your own mini-story! 📝

Write 3–4 short sentences about a hungry animal and food. Use First, Then, After that, and Finally.

Helpful words: First • Then • After that • Finally • ate • liked • apple • pear • strawberry • orange • cake • butterfly
